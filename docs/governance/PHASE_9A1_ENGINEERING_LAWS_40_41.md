**Program:** Mira Face Analysis Experience Transformation

## LAW #40 — APPROVED (presentation program)
**EVERY FACE VISUALIZATION MUST HAVE A TRUTH CLASSIFICATION.**  
No visual component may imply greater measurement precision than the underlying evidence.

## LAW #41 — APPROVED (presentation program)
**MIRA MAY ENHANCE THE EXPERIENCE OF ANALYSIS, BUT ANIMATION MUST NEVER PRETEND TO PERFORM AN ANALYSIS THE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM DID NOT PERFORM.**

Applies to: laser, scan, mesh, depth, heatmap, region activation, progress animation.

Compatible with ADR-FI-003 / ADR-FI-005. Does not modify Face Intelligence engines.  
Adoption scope: Face Analysis Experience Transformation program (presentation layer).
