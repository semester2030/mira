# FK-7 — Ownership Boundary

**Owns:** fabric/texture/silhouette/proportion knowledge interpretation, garment visual volume, length relationships, layering *styling knowledge* (not structural legality), formality implications of materials/silhouettes (qualified).

**Does not own:** garment detection, body analysis, pose, measurements, body-shape classification, attractiveness, CanonicalGarment mutation, outfit scoring, styling preference ownership, Advisor narration, product recommendation, OI Layering Engine.
