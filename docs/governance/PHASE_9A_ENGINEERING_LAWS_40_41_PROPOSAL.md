## Candidate LAW #40
Every face visualization must declare whether it represents measured, derived, inferred, illustrative, or decorative information.

## Candidate LAW #41
Mira may enhance the experience of analysis, but animation must never pretend to perform an analysis the intelligence system did not perform.

Compatible with ADR-FI-003/005 and Skin map truth labeling.
