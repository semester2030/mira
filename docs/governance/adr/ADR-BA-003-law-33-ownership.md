# ADR-BA-003 — Engineering Law #33 (Ownership)

**Status:** Accepted · Frozen in AI Beauty Advisor v1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

Advisor must not become a parallel intelligence stack.

## Decision

**Law #33:** AI Beauty Advisor never replaces frozen intelligence.

Advisor owns conversation, planning, routing, envelope, memory orchestration only. Analysis/evaluation remain upstream frozen subsystems.

## Consequences

- No Skin/Face/GI/OI/Styling re-evaluation inside Advisor.  
- Weakening Law #33 requires MAJOR + Independent review.
