# ADR-OI-003 — Engineering Law #31 for Outfit Intelligence

**Status:** Accepted · Frozen in Outfit Intelligence v1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

Outfit evaluation must be evidence-backed and auditable.

## Decision

Law #31 applies fully to CanonicalOutfit evaluation:

- Every metric references evidence.  
- Every confidence references evidence.  
- Explainability references evidence.  
- No uncited evidence on full evaluation.  
- Multi-record graphs must be connected; edges must be valid.  
- Completeness claims must be evidence-honest.

Capability-only paths enforce graph integrity (not full citation, because no CanonicalOutfit metrics).

## Consequences

- Validators are part of the protected surface.  
- Weakening Law #31 requires CR + Independent review.
